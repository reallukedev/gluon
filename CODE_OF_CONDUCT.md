# Code of conduct

Gluon follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good intent, keep criticism about the work, and don't harass anyone.
Everyone is welcome here regardless of who they are or how much they know.

To report a problem, use [GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/slukedev/gluon/security/advisories/new)
or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are kept confidential.
