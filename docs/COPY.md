# Copy

How Gluon talks. Short, calm and exact. If a screen needs a paragraph to make sense, fix the screen.

## Say less

- A page title plus one sentence of state. Add a second sentence only when it holds the fix.
- Say each fact once per screen. If the header says "Nothing needs you", the panel below doesn't repeat it.
- Don't explain what Gluon does in general ("Gluon checks disks around the clock"). Show it.
- Help text under a field only when the label can't carry it alone. One line.
- Long explanations go behind a "How this works" disclosure, or away.
- Empty states: one line on what goes here, one button to start. No pep talk.
- Leave out "Gluon" as the subject when the sentence works without it ("Couldn't reach the router", not "Gluon couldn't reach the router").

## Words

- Sentence case everywhere. Numbers as figures ("3 apps").
- Buttons name the action, verb first, usually two words: "Restart Jellyfin", "Add drive", "Free 5.2 GB". Never "Submit", "OK" or "Yes".
- Icon buttons have a tooltip that names the action, nothing else.
- Errors say what happened, then what to do: "Couldn't save. The disk is full; free some space and try again."
- Confirmations list consequences in plain words, then name the action on the button.
- Household members never see jargon they can't act on (containers, ports, compose, cgroups).
- Product words: app (not stack or service, unless on the Docker tab), drive, folder, address, sign in, two-step.

## Never

- Em dashes. Use a period, comma or colon.
- Eyebrow labels above headings, or section numbers.
- Exclamation marks, "simply", "just", "easily", "seamless", "powerful".
- Filler stats or the same number said two ways ("9 apps · All 9 · 9 running").
